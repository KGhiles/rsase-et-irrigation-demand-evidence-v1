# Scope and interpretation

The archived source tables support the manuscript's analyses for 2011, 2015,
2017 and 2023. The fixed 2011 corn-object frame is held constant across years;
annual CDL masks define separate year-specific crop populations. Results from
these populations are not interchangeable.

The fixed frame contains 1,184 objects. Removing 2011 from the four-year mean
produced 1,139 strong dominance cases (absolute change at least 20%), zero
moderate cases (10% to less than 20%), one low case (less than 10%) and 44
cases without a computable percentage. These categories describe relative
changes in an efficiency-scaled deficit proxy, not observed irrigation.

The known-cold experiment measured the sensitivity of accepted METRIC output
to a different cold reference on supported Landsat dates. These diagnostic
re-solves were not substituted into the published seasonal ET estimates.

The package contains source tables for the accepted figures and comparisons.
It does not provide a complete rebuild of the original satellite-processing
workflow, independent field validation or proof of irrigation/pumping.
